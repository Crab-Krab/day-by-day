The first stable release of Day by Day: a daily checklist that runs directly from one HTML file.

## Download and use
1. Under Assets, download **Day-by-Day.html**.
2. Open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another modern browser.

No installation, server, account, or internet connection is needed for the app.

## Included
- Daily checklists, weekday navigation, and a month calendar.
- One-time tasks and weekly routines on multiple selected days, including a Mon–Fri shortcut.
- Independent completion for each scheduled date.
- All-day tasks or specific local times, with 5, 15, 30, and 60-minute reminders.
- Up/down ordering controls beside each checkbox.
- Light/dark themes and subtle animations, with smoother day switching and reduced-motion support.
- Automatic browser-local saving and JSON backup/restore.

## Data and reminders
Your tasks stay in your browser. Back up tasks before replacing or moving your working file, changing browsers, or clearing browser data.

Keep the file open and your computer awake for reminders. Desktop notifications are optional and depend on browser permissions and workplace policies. In-app reminders are included.
